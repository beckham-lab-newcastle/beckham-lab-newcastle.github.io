Kate and PhD student Harry Flower spent a few days in Paris this month for the [EMBO Workshop on Mycobacterial Infections](https://meetings.embo.org/event/26-mycobacterial-infections).

Harry presented a poster on our early work characterising a novel MspA-like outer membrane complex in *M. abscessus*, and had a steady stream of visitors to talk through the data.

![A crowd gathered around Harry's poster during the session]({{ '/assets/images/embo-mycobacterial-2026-poster-session.jpg' | relative_url }})

Great few days of talks, posters and catching up with the mycobacterial infections community — rounded off with a rooftop view over Paris at sunset.
