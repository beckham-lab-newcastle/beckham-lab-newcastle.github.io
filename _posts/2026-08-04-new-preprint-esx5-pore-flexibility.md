A new preprint from Christina Ritter, Grzegorz Chojnowski and colleagues at EMBL Hamburg, with Kate as a co-author, is now up on bioRxiv: [*Intrinsic flexibility of Type VII secretion central pore is required for substrate translocation*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.31.742005v1).

It follows on from earlier work on the ESX-5 pore complex and shows that flexibility in the pore-forming helices of EccC5 is needed for substrates to get through the mycobacterial type VII secretion system.

Read the preprint on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.31.742005v1).
